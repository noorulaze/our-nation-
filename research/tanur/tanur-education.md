# Tanur (താനൂർ) — Education, Schools, Colleges & Literacy

**Investigator:** Nammude Naadu Research & Verification Desk  
**Data Scope:** Higher Education, Theological Academies, Vocational Institutions, Secondary Schools, Literacy Profile  
**Verification Level:** High (Cross-verified with University of Calicut, General Education Dept, Fisheries Dept, and Islahul Uloom Annals)

---

## 1. Literacy & Educational Overview

- **Literacy Rate (Census 2011):** **91.18%**
  - Male Literacy: **94.15%**
  - Female Literacy: **88.55%**
- **Educational Context:** Tanur has experienced a dramatic educational transformation over the past century. Initially rooted in traditional temple patasalas and mosque-based Dars institutions, the town pioneered early 20th-century institutional educational models through leaders like **Pangil Ahmed Kutty Musliyar** and public school benefactors, later bolstered by state government infrastructure and diaspora philanthropy.

## 2. Higher Education & Collegiate Institutions

### 2.1 C.H. Muhammed Koya Memorial Govt. Arts and Science College, Tanur
- **Official Website:** `gctanur.ac.in`
- **Affiliation:** University of Calicut
- **Year of Establishment:** **2013**
- **Location:** Puthentheru, Tanur (approx. 2 km from the town center).
- **Academic Programs Offered:**
  - Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in English Language and Literature.
  - Bachelor of Business Administration (B.B.A.).
  - Bachelor of Computer Applications (B.C.A.).
  - Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Electronics.
  - Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) with Computer Applications.
  - Integrated Master of Arts (M.A.) in Malayalam.
- **Significance:** Established by the Government of Kerala to meet the long-standing demand for public higher education in coastal Malappuram, particularly serving female students from rural and fisherfolk backgrounds.

---

### 2.2 Islahul Uloom Arabic College (ഇസ്ലാഹുൽ ഉലൂം അറബിക് കോളേജ്)
- **Official Website:** `islahululoom.in`
- **Year of Establishment:** **October 1924** (Centenary commemorated in 2024).
- **Founder:** **Pangil Ahmed Kutty Musliyar** (1888–1946), legendary religious scholar and first President of Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama.
- **Academic Scope:**
  - Offers classical Afzal-ul-Ulama degree courses affiliated with the University of Calicut alongside traditional Islamic theology and Shafi'i jurisprudence.
  - Focuses on advanced Arabic linguistics, Quranic exegesis (Tafsir), Hadith sciences, and classical logic.
- **Manuscript Archives:** Houses a notable collection of hand-copied Arabic and Arabi-Malayalam manuscripts, historic fatwas, and colonial-era religious treatises.

## 3. Specialized & Technical Institutions

### 3.1 Govt. Regional Fisheries Technical Vocational Higher Secondary School (RFTVHSS), Tanur
- **Administrative Department:** Directorate of Fisheries, Government of Kerala.
- **Focus:** Specialized technical education geared towards empowering youth from traditional coastal and fishing families.
- **Vocational Streams:**
  - Marine Fisheries & Navigation.
  - Aquaculture & Fish Hatchery Operations.
  - Seafood Processing & Quality Assurance.
- **Sports Training Wing:** The school functions as a regional coastal sports talent hub, fielding recognized teams in athletics, martial arts (judo and wushu), and water sports.

## 4. Prominent Secondary & Higher Secondary Schools

### 4.1 Devadhar Govt. Higher Secondary School (DGHSS)
- **Status:** Historic Premier Institution of the Tanur/Tanalur Region.
- **Location:** Tanalur (adjoining Tanur Municipality; located within the Tanur Block).
- **Establishment:** **1922–1924** (operations traceable locally to 1919 under British patronage and regional philanthropic sponsorship).
- **Evolution:** Upgraded to Higher Secondary status in **1990**.
- **Campus & Facilities:**
  - Caters to thousands of students from Class 5 through Class 12 across Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams.
  - Equipped with modern smart classrooms, high-tech science laboratories, computer labs, an extensive library, and expansive sports grounds.
  - Designated as a recognized Skill Development Centre (SDC) under Kerala's Additional Skill Acquisition Programme (ASAP).

### 4.2 Other Secondary & English Medium Schools
- **Govt. High School Tanur Town:** Primary and secondary public education campus.
- **Aided Mappila High Schools:** Network of government-aided schools established under community educational trusts.
- **Private & CBSE Schools:** Crescent English Medium School, Al-Farook English Medium Higher Secondary School, offering Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) curricula.

---

## 5. Non-Formal, Religious & Community Learning

- **Madrasa Education:** The widespread network of primary Madrasas administered by the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board provides ethical and religious education to Muslim children in morning shifts before regular secular school hours.
- **State Literacy Mission & Akshaya Centers:** Tanur's network of Akshaya e-centers and municipal continuing education cells actively conduct digital literacy drives, senior citizen literacy programs, and equivalency examinations (4th, 7th, 10th, and 12th standard equivalents).
