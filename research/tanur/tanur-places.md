# Tanur (താനൂർ) — Landmarks, Heritage Sites & Geography

**Investigator:** Nammude Naadu Research & Verification Desk  
**Data Category:** Physical Landmarks, Heritage Monuments, Coastal Infrastructure  
**Verification Level:** High (Cross-verified with Kerala Tourism, LSGD, Devaswom, and Satellite Coordinates)

---

## 1. Overview of Geography & Physical Terrain

- **Coordinates:** 10.9768° N, 75.8675° E
- **Elevation:** Average 3 to 7 metres above mean sea level (coastal sandy littoral plain).
- **Physical Boundaries:**
  - **West:** Arabian Sea (approx. 8.5 km unbroken coastal shoreline).
  - **North:** Parappanangadi Municipality & Vallikkunnu (separated in part by the Poorappuzha estuary).
  - **East:** Thanalur and Ozhur Grama Panchayats.
  - **South:** Tirur Municipality and coastal Niramaruthur.
- **Rivers & Waterways:** The **Poorappuzha River** (a coastal tributary linked to the Kadalundi drainage system) flows westward and empties into the Arabian Sea at the northern boundary of the town at **Ottumpuram/Thooval Theeram**.

## 2. Comprehensive Inventory of Landmarks

### 2.1 Tanur Beach (താനൂർ കടൽത്തീരം)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9768° N, 75.8560° E
- **Category:** Coastal Beach & Public Promenade
- **Significance:** The central recreational coastline of the town. Characterised by wide golden sandy beaches, coconut groves, and casuarina plantations. In the evening, it serves as a gathering space for residents and visitors witnessing Arabian Sea sunsets.
- **Features:** Concrete beach seating benches, walkway, street food kiosks (selling fried seafood, pickled fruit, Kallummakkaya / green mussels, and spiced snacks), and safe landing spots for local country fishing craft.
- **Visitor Timings:** Open 24 hours; peak visiting hours 4:30 PM – 7:30 PM.
- **Source:** Kerala Tourism & Local Municipal records.

---

### 2.2 Tanur Fishing Harbour & Breakwater (താനൂർ ഫിഷിംഗ് ഹാർബർ & പുലിമുട്ട്)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9740° N, 75.8520° E
- **Category:** Maritime Infrastructure & Economic Center
- **Significance:** A major modern fishing harbour constructed by the Harbour Engineering Department (HED), Government of Kerala. It accommodates hundreds of traditional inboard/outboard fishing boats and small trawlers.
- **Key Infrastructure:**
  - **Pulimuttu (Breakwater):** Massive granite rock groynes extending deep into the Arabian Sea to protect the harbour basin from heavy wave surges. Visitors frequently walk along the breakwater pathway during non-monsoon calm weather for panoramic maritime views.
  - **Auction Halls:** Spacious hygienic landing wharves where pre-dawn and afternoon fresh fish auctions take place.
  - **Ice Plants & Cold Storage:** Supporting commercial preservation and transport across South India.
- **Visitor Guidelines:** Early morning (5:30 AM – 8:00 AM) is optimal to witness the vibrant fish auction; strictly adhere to warning signs during heavy southwest monsoon swells.
- **Source:** Kerala Fisheries Department; Harbour Engineering Department Reports.

### 2.3 Keraladeshapuram Temple (കേരളാദിത്യപുരം / കേരളദേശപുരം ശ്രീകൃഷ്ണ ക്ഷേത്രം)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9520° N, 75.8690° E (approx. 3 km south of Tanur Town center)
- **Category:** Ancient Hindu Temple / Heritage Monument
- **Presiding Deity:** Lord Vishnu / Sree Krishna (worshipped in four-armed chaturbahu form).
- **Historical Significance:**
  - Widely regarded by historians and temple chroniclers as **one of the oldest temples in Kerala**, with origins tracing back over a millennium to the Kulasekhara Chera period.
  - Served as the royal tutelary temple of the **Vettathiri Rajas of Vettathunadu**.
  - Visited in **1546 by St. Francis Xavier**, who recorded observations about its rituals and architecture.
  - During the Mysore invasions (Tipu Sultan's army), local traditions record that the chief idol was concealed in a temple well to protect it from destruction, and was later consecrated anew.
- **Architectural Highlights:** Classic Kerala dvitala sreekovil with copper sheet roofing, stone inscriptions, traditional chuttambalam, and ornate wood carvings depicting puranic episodes.
- **Key Festivals:** Annual Aarattu / Pooram festival in the Malayalam month of Vrischikam (November–December), featuring temple percussion (Panchavadyam, Melam) and classical ceremonies.
- **Visitor Timings:** Morning 5:00 AM – 11:30 AM; Evening 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM.
- **Source:** Department of Tourism, Govt. of Kerala (Destination ID 338); Malabar Devaswom Board.

### 2.4 Ottumpuram Beach & Thooval Theeram (ഒട്ടുംപുറം തൂവൽതീരം)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9920° N, 75.8480° E
- **Category:** Estuarine Ecotourism & Beach Landscape
- **Significance:** Located at the northern coastal border where the Poorappuzha River enters the Arabian Sea. The confluence creates an expansive sandbar, calm riverine lagoons on the eastern flank, and open surf on the west.
- **Ecosystem:** Fringed by coastal mangrove stands, mudflats, and migratory bird feeding grounds. Common avian species include black-headed gulls, sandpipers, egrets, and river terns.
- **Historical & Modern Context:** Developed as an ecotourism promenade with children's play areas, mangrove walkways, and recreational boat services. In May 2023, the estuary witnessed a tragic boat mishap; subsequently, the Kerala Maritime Board and Tourism Department implemented strict boating safety certifications, life-jacket regulations, and restricted navigational hours.
- **Source:** Kerala Tourism Coastal Circuit Development; District Administration Reports.

### 2.5 Shobhaparambu Sree Kurumba Bhagavathi Temple (ശോഭപറമ്പ് ശ്രീ കുരുംബ ഭഗവതി ക്ഷേത്രം)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9850° N, 75.8750° E
- **Category:** Ancient Devi Shrine / Folk Heritage Center
- **Presiding Deity:** Bhadrakali (worshipped in a tranquil, benevolent form as *Santha Swaroopini*).
- **Ritual Lineage:** Priestly administration has historically been overseen by the **Valiyapuraakkal Aven** family.
- **The Kalankari Maholsavam:**
  - The signature annual festival celebrated in late December (Dhanu month).
  - Famous for **Kaalavarav** (elaborately decorated artificial bull pageants sponsored by local desoms/wards), **Singari Melam**, and **Kavadiyattam**.
  - Features the grand **Thalappoli** procession where hundreds of women dressed in traditional Kerala kasavu attire carry oil lamps in procession around the temple.
  - Includes archaic ritual offerings: *Puthan Kalam* (new earthen pot nivedyam using fresh harvest rice) dedicated to the sub-deity *Dandan*, and midnight *Guruthi pooja*.
- **Official Website/Records:** `sobhaparambubhagavathi.com`; Department of Culture research papers.

### 2.6 Islahul Uloom Arabic College (ഇസ്ലാഹുൽ ഉലൂം അറബിക് കോളേജ്)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9780° N, 75.8690° E
- **Category:** Historic Educational Institution & Theological College
- **Founding Date:** **October 1924** (centenary observed in 2024).
- **Founder:** **Pangil Ahmed Kutty Musliyar** (1888–1946), legendary religious reformer, freedom-era leader, and founding President of Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama.
- **Significance:** One of the earliest formalized Islamic higher educational academies in northern Kerala, blending traditional Dars scholarship with structured Arabic linguistics and Islamic jurisprudence. The college played a vital role in institutionalising modern higher education within the Malabar Muslim community in the post-1921 era.
- **Campus:** Houses historic library archives of rare Arabic manuscripts, lecture halls, and residential hostels.
- **Source:** Official Islahul Uloom Governing Body (`islahululoom.in`); Samastha Golden Jubilee Volumes.

### 2.7 Tanur Town Juma Masjid (താനൂർ ടൗൺ ജുമാ മസ്ജിദ്)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9760° N, 75.8640° E
- **Category:** Historic Mosque & Civic Centre
- **Significance:** Situated in the heart of Tanur Angadi. Historically connected to the merchant guilds of coastal Malabar. Characterised by traditional Kerala-Islamic wooden architecture, tiered gables, hand-carved teak beams, and an adjoining historic cemetery containing tombs of coastal spiritual figures.
- **Source:** Tanur Muslim Orphanage & Mahal Committee Records.

### 2.8 Tanur Railway Station (താനൂർ റെയിൽവേ സ്റ്റേഷൻ - TA)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9772° N, 75.8670° E
- **Category:** Railway Heritage & Transport Gateway
- **Station Code:** `TA` (Palakkad Division, Southern Railway).
- **Historical Milestone:** Sits along Kerala’s first railway line, opened on **March 12, 1861** between Tirur and Beypore.
- **Modern Facilities:** Two active electrified broad-gauge platforms, daily stoppage for passenger and express trains (including Malabar Express, Parasuram Express, Mangalore–Coimbatore Express, and Netravati Express), computerized passenger reservation system, and foot-overbridge.
- **Source:** Southern Railway Historical Timetables; Ministry of Railways.

### 2.9 Thrikkaikkattu Shiva Temple (തൃക്കൈക്കാട്ട് ശിവക്ഷേത്രം)
- **Coordinates:** 10.9680° N, 75.8720° E
- **Category:** Ancient Shiva Temple
- **Significance:** Dedicated to Lord Shiva (Kirathamoorthy concept). Associated with traditional Namboothiri illams of Vettathunadu. Features circular stone sanctum (vatta sreekovil) and sacred temple tank. Celebrates Maha Shivaratri with special dhara and night-long vigils.
- **Source:** Malabar Devaswom Directory.

---

### 2.10 Rayirimangalam & Ganapathiyangadi Heritage Enclave
- **Coordinates:** 10.9810° N, 75.8710° E
- **Category:** Cultural Street & Traditional Artisan Quarter
- **Significance:** Ancient settlement pocket of gold artisans, blacksmiths, and traditional traders. Houses the Rayirimangalam Ganapathi Temple and historic stone wells used by coastal caravans since medieval times.
- **Source:** Tanur Local History Society.
