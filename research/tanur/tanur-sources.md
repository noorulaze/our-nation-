## 2. Historical Texts, Archives & Scholarly Publications

### 2.1 *Malabar Manual* (Vols. I & II) — William Logan
- **Author:** William Logan (Collector of Malabar, Madras Civil Service)
- **Publisher / Year:** Madras Government Press, 1887 (Reprinted by Kerala Gazetteers Dept)
- **Category:** Primary Historical Gazette
- **Facts Verified:**
  - Kingdom of Vettathunadu (Tanur Swaroopam), royal titles, and relationship with the Zamorin of Calicut.
  - French factory / loge established in Tanur in 1739.
  - Death of the last Vettathu Raja on **May 24, 1793** and the escheat of the kingdom to the British East India Company under the Treaty of Seringapatam framework.
  - 1861 railway extension details.

### 2.2 Monumenta Xaveriana & Jesuit Missionary Correspondence
- **Author/Editors:** Monumenta Historica Societatis Iesu (MHSI), Rome
- **Historical Period:** 1542–1552 (Letters of St. Francis Xavier)
- **Category:** 16th-Century Primary Correspondence
- **Facts Verified:**
  - Xavier's journey along the Malabar Coast in 1546 and documented stay at Tanur.
  - 1549 conversion of the King of Tanur (baptized Dom João) and Portuguese naval diplomacy surrounding Chaliyam.

### 2.3 *Islam in Kerala: History and Culture*
- **Scholarly Repository:** Academic publications and annals of Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama
- **Category:** Regional Cultural & Religious History
- **Facts Verified:**
  - Founding of **Islahul Uloom Arabic College** in **October 1924** by **Pangil Ahmed Kutty Musliyar**.
  - Socio-educational conferences held at Waliya Kulangara Masjid, Tanur, post the 1921 Malabar Rebellion.

### 2.4 Imperial Police & District Magistrate Reports (1921)
- **Source:** National Archives of India; Tamil Nadu State Archives (Madras Judicial Proceedings)
- **Historical Period:** August–December 1921
- **Category:** Archival Records of the 1921 Malabar Rebellion
- **Facts Verified:**
  - Sabotage of the railway line between Tanur and Parappanangadi/Tirur by Khilafat volunteers.
  - British military deployments and subsequent detainee roundups along coastal Tanur leading up to the November 1921 Wagon Tragedy.
