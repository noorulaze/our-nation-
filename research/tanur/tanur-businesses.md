## 1. The Marine Fisheries Economy

### 1.1 Landing Infrastructure & Operations
- Tanur represents one of the most critical marine fish landing centers on the Malabar Coast between Beypore and Ponnani.
- **The Tanur Fishing Harbour Basin:**
  - Designed with twin granite rubble-mound breakwaters (*Pulimuttu*) that provide tranquil berthing conditions for country craft, fiber-reinforced plastic (FRP) boats, and motorized ring-seiners.
  - Employs over **5,000 active traditional fishermen** directly and supports an estimated **15,000 livelihoods** indirectly across processing, packaging, logistics, and retail.
- **Major Commercial Marine Species Landed:**
  - *Mathi (Indian Oil Sardine - Sardinella longiceps)*: The volume backbone of the local fisheries economy.
  - *Ayala (Indian Mackerel - Rastrelliger kanagurta)*.
  - *Neymeen / Aikoora (Seer Fish / King Mackerel)*.
  - *Vala (Ribbonfish)*.
  - *Chemeen (Prawns / Shrimps)*: Tiger prawns and white prawns captured during bottom-trawl and estuarine operations.
  - *Koonthal & Kanava (Squid and Cuttlefish)*: High-value export commodities.
  - *Kallummakkaya (Green Mussels - Perna viridis)*: Harvested from coastal rock beds.

### 1.2 Cold Chain Logistics & Export Distribution
- **Ice Production:** Tanur hosts multiple private and cooperative ice plants producing block ice and crushed flake ice to chill fish immediately upon landing at the harbour.
- **Inter-State Reefer Logistics:**
  - Insulated and refrigerated trucks operate daily from Tanur, transporting fresh catch overnight to interior Kerala markets (Palakkad, Thrissur, Ernakulam, Wayanad) and major metropolitan terminals in **Tamil Nadu (Coimbatore, Pollachi, Madurai, Chennai)** and **Karnataka (Bangalore, Mysore)**.
- **Export Processing:** High-grade squids, cuttlefish, and large ribbonfish are routed to marine seafood export factories in Cochin and Mangalore for shipment to European, East Asian, and Middle Eastern markets.

### 1.3 Dry Fish Curing & Processing
- Tanur has a historical reputation for salted dried fish (*Unakka Meen*).
- **Processing Yards:** Modernization initiatives have introduced solar tunnel driers alongside traditional raised bamboo drying racks to ensure hygienic processing of anchovies (*Netholi*), sardines, and ribbonfish without sand contamination.

## 2. Ancillary Maritime Industries

### 2.1 Boat Building & Marine Mechanical Yards
- **Fiberglass Boat Fabrication:** Several specialized boatyards located along Beach Road manufacture and repair traditional motorized canoes (*Vallangal*) and fiberglass speed crafts.
- **Marine Engine Workshops:** Outboard motor (OBM) service workshops specialize in maintenance and overhaul of Yamaha, Suzuki, and Tohatsu marine engines.
- **Net Weaving & Rigging:** Traditional artisanal net mending works alongside retail stores supplying monofilament nylon nets, ring-seine webbing, floats, sinkers, and marine-grade nylon ropes.

## 3. Traditional Agricultural & Agro-Processing Trade

### 3.1 Coconut & Copra Processing
- Coconut cultivation forms the historical backbone of Tanur's hinterland agriculture.
- **Copra Yards:** Tanur features copra drying units where split coconuts are sun-dried or hot-air dried, then crushed in local expeller mills to produce pure, unrefined coconut oil and oil cake (*Pinnakku*) for cattle feed.

### 3.2 Coir & Fibre Extraction
- Historically, the backwaters and lagoons of the Poorappuzha estuary were utilized for natural retting of coconut husks.
- While mechanized defibering units have largely superseded traditional retting, cottage-scale coir spinning and coir geotextile weaving persist in several suburban wards.

### 3.3 Betel Leaf (Tirur Vettila) Trade
- The surrounding region of Tanur and Tirur produces the famous **Tirur Betel Leaf (തിരൂർ വെറ്റില)**, which holds a prestigious **Geographical Indication (GI) tag** due to its distinctive pungency, high essential oil content, and extended shelf-life. Local farmers trade their betel vine harvests through regional agricultural collection centers in Tanur and Tirur for export to northern India and Pakistan.

## 4. Retail Corridors & Commercial Centers

### 4.1 Tanur Town Angadi (Main Market Corridor)
- The historic commercial core stretching along Main Road and Mosque Road.
- Characterized by multi-storey commercial complexes housing textile emporiums, Malabar bridal jewellery showrooms, footwear stores, electronic retailers, and grocery markets.

### 4.2 Station Road Commercial Spine
- The trade artery connecting Tanur Town with Tanur Railway Station.
- Concentrated with wholesale grain merchants, provision stores, hardware and building material depots, sanitaryware outlets, and parcel logistics booking offices.

### 4.3 Harbour Road (Fisheries Corridor)
- Directly connects town with the fishing harbour.
- Dominated by wholesale fish auction offices, marine equipment outlets, diesel bunkering stations, ice factories, and driver rest areas.

## 5. Banking & Financial Ecosystem

- **Public Sector Banks:** State Bank of India (SBI - Tanur Branch), Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank.
- **Regional Rural Banks:** Kerala Gramin Bank (Tanur Branch).
- **Private Scheduled Banks:** Federal Bank, South Indian Bank.
- **Cooperative Banking:** The Tanur Service Co-operative Bank and several primary agricultural credit societies (PACS) offering agricultural micro-credit, gold loans, and fisheries equipment subsidies.
